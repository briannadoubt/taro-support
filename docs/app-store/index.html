Taro Reader support

Help with the App Store edition of Taro Reader.

Find card meanings

Open the Library to browse the 78 Rider–Waite cards and look up their meanings, descriptions, and keywords.

Read physical cards

The optional AR Reader is available on iPhone and iPad for the physical Rider–Waite deck. If the camera does not open, check that Taro Reader has camera permission in Settings.

Troubleshoot sync or missing images

If a reading is missing on another device, check that both devices use the same Apple Account and can access iCloud. If a card shows only its back, check your internet connection and reopen the reading. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting a reading you want to keep.

Contact support

Email bri@briannadoubt.com for help or a privacy question. For a bug, please include the device, OS version, app version, and steps to reproduce it. Please don’t send passwords, Apple Account credentials, or private readings.
