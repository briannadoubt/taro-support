Privacy policy

Taro Reader 2.0

Updated October 3, 2026

Data collection

Taro Reader does not include advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics services. The current app does not send your readings or conversations to a developer-operated server. No app account is required.

Data stored in the app

The app stores readings, including titles, dates, layouts, cards and orientations; optional recipient names and zodiac signs; and interpretation conversations. Associated speech data may also be stored. Your chosen profile name and sign may be saved in device preferences.

iCloud synchronization

Readings can sync through Apple’s private CloudKit database using your Apple Account. If iCloud is unavailable, the app can use local storage instead. Apple operates iCloud; its handling of Apple Account and service data is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

On-device interpretations

Optional interpretations use Apple Foundation Models on supported devices. Your text is processed on the device rather than sent to an app-operated inference service. This requires a compatible device and Apple Intelligence with its model available.

Camera

The optional physical-card reader uses camera access for ARKit image tracking on supported iPhone and iPad devices. Card recognition happens on the device. The audited app has no service that uploads camera images to the developer.

SharePlay

If you explicitly opt in to share a session profile, your chosen display name and zodiac sign are transmitted through Apple GroupActivities to session participants. Reading notes and interpretation conversations are not included in that profile payload.

Your choices

You can leave recipient and profile details blank, decline camera access, and decline profile sharing. Device privacy settings control camera permission. Manage iCloud access through your Apple Account and device settings. Reading changes may sync to your other devices.

Support messages

If you email support, the information you choose to send is available to the recipient to answer your request. Avoid including private readings or conversations unless they are necessary to describe the problem.

This website

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics script or tracking pixel added by Taro’s developer. GitHub operates the hosting service; see GitHub’s privacy statement for its handling of website requests.
