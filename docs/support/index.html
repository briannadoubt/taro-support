Taro Reader support

Help with the card library, readings, and optional interpretations.

Explore the library

The Library contains all 78 Rider–Waite cards, with artwork, upright and reversed meanings, keywords, and descriptions. Card artwork is included with the app.

Create a reading

Open Readings and create a new reading. Choose an included layout, such as Three Card, Five Card, or Celtic Cross, then draw cards. Select a card to explore its details. You can choose an optional recipient name and zodiac sign.

Earlier scanned readings keep their cards and orientations. They may appear as freeform readings when no spread positions were stored.

Use interpretations

Optional interpretations require a compatible device running iOS 26, macOS 26, or visionOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence enabled and its model available. If the feature is unavailable, check device support and Apple Intelligence settings. The card library and reading tools remain available.

Interpretations are generated on your device. Generated responses can be mistaken; use your own judgment when considering them.

Sync with iCloud

Use the same Apple Account on your devices and make sure iCloud is available. Allow time for readings to sync. If iCloud cannot open, Taro may save readings locally without syncing. Avoid deleting the app or its data while troubleshooting a reading you want to keep.

Recognize physical cards

On iPhone and iPad, the optional camera-based reader recognizes supported physical Rider–Waite cards on devices that support image tracking. Allow camera access when using this feature. Creating readings and browsing the library do not require the camera.

Share a session

SharePlay can share your optional chosen name and zodiac sign with session participants when you opt in. Check your choices before sharing a profile.

Optional tips

Tips are unavailable in this release. No purchase is required to use Taro Reader.

Contact support

Email bri@briannadoubt.com. Include your device model, operating-system version, app version, and the steps that caused the problem. You do not need to send a private reading, conversation, password, or Apple Account credentials.
